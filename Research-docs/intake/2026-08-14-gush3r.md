## What it is

**GUSH3R (Gaussian-Unified Scene Human 3D Reconstruction)** — "Everyone Everywhere All at Once as Gaussians". A **feed-forward** framework for **online dynamic human-scene reconstruction from a single monocular video**: dynamic humans + static scene reconstructed in a single forward pass as 3DGS primitives, geometrically consistent, capable of novel view synthesis. Claims competitive NVS quality with much better inference efficiency than optimization-based methods.

- Authors: Keito Abe, Kaede Shiohara (project lead), Takashi Otonari, Toshihiko Yamasaki — University of Tokyo
- Paper: arXiv:2607.05243 (July 2026) · Project page: https://abkeito.github.io/gush3r-page/
- Lineage (per upstream README acknowledgements): built on **Human3R** (its `src/` is adopted from it, MIT), related to CUT3R, AnySplat, BEDLAM; optional TTT3R recurrent update (`--use_ttt3r`)
- Stack: PyTorch 2.2 + CUDA 12.1, PyTorch3D, `ashawkey/diff-gaussian-rasterization`, MultiHMR for human pose, SMPL/SMPL-X body models, DUSt3R/CroCo backbone

## Why it may matter for Shramko-Human-4D (hypotheses to research)

1. **Monocular feed-forward front-end.** Variant 1 assumes per-capture 4D optimization from the 2× GoPro rig. GUSH3R produces human+scene 3DGS from ONE camera in a forward pass — could serve as a fast initializer / prior for the per-capture fit, cutting optimization time (cf. v6 6.16 zero-training-MVP and 6.18 camera-frontend).
2. **Human/background separation for free.** It explicitly splits dynamic human Gaussians from accumulated static background Gaussians (`--bg_*` options) — relevant to our capture pipeline's matting/segmentation stage.
3. **SMPL-X-anchored HumanGS sampling cache** — a concrete recipe for binding Gaussians to a parametric body; compare with our kinematics plan (v6 6.3).
4. **Benchmark/baseline.** As a published monocular baseline, useful for honesty in our public claims ("what does a 1-camera SOTA already give for free vs. our multi-camera rig").
