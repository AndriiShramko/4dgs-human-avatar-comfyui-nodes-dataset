## 20.0 The core technical truth (read before anything)

The product uses **2 frontal cameras at inference**. But to *train* a model that invents the unseen back, the **training data must contain the back** — there is no other source of supervision for it. So:

- **Inference geometry**: 2 cameras, both facing the front (stereo-ish), giving real depth + extra detail on the visible side. Back unobserved → generated.
- **Training geometry**: a **surround rig** (cameras all the way around, including the direct back + a couple elevated). During training, the **2 frontal cameras are the "reference" slots** (N_ref=2 in PRoPE terms, §8.1.1) and **every other camera — including the back — is a "target" the model must learn to predict**. This is exactly the Flex4DHuman curriculum with the reference set fixed to a frontal pair.
- **Train/inference geometry MUST match**: the 2 reference cameras in training must sit at the *same* relative pose the product will use (height, baseline, distance, azimuth), or the model sees a distribution shift at deploy time.

This is why §6.17's surround datasets (PKU-DyMVHumans 56-60 cams, CMU Panoptic, AIST++) are the backbone of training: they already have back views — we just subsample 2 frontal cams as the reference and supervise on all the rest. Own-shooting (below) is for domain alignment to the exact product rig.

## 20.2 Detailed own-data capture spec (the "what and how to shoot")

Use this only for **domain alignment** after pre-training on §6.17's surround datasets — it makes the model match *your* exact product rig.

**Rig geometry**
- **Surround coverage**: ≥8 cameras (viable) / 12-16 (ideal) evenly around the subject at chest height, **including one pointed at the direct back** (180°), plus 2 elevated (~30-45°) for top-of-head/shoulders. More cameras = denser back GT = better hallucination quality.
- **The reference pair (must mirror the product)**: 2 cameras both frontal, at **chest height (~1.4 m)**, **baseline ~0.6-1.0 m apart**, **~2.5-3 m from subject**, each ~**±15° azimuth** from dead-center. These two are what the product will use at inference — document their exact pose and reuse it verbatim in deployment.
- All cameras **hardware/timecode synchronized** (your iPhone+Tentacle or GoPro-Labs experience applies); **fixed** (no handheld); **calibrated** each session with a charuco/checkerboard pass → intrinsics + extrinsics for every camera.

**Subjects & variation (this is what teaches the back)**
- **20-50+ people**: diverse body types, heights, ages, skin tones.
- **Clothing chosen for back variety**: loose tops, jackets, dresses, hoods, long hair (up and down), hats, **backpacks**, **printed logos/text on the back** — the model only learns to invent backs it has seen the distribution of.
- **Motion per take**: a **slow full 360° self-turn** (most important — gives every camera a clean look at the back and teaches back appearance), then arm raises, walking in place, sitting down/standing, gesturing, hair movement.
- **Takes**: 10-20 s each, several per subject, varied poses.

**Capture settings**
- **Resolution**: ≥1080p everywhere; **4K on the 2 frontal cameras** (front detail is the selling point).
- **Frame rate**: ≥24-30 fps, consistent across cameras.
- **Lighting**: one even/soft set (clean GT) + optionally one varied set for robustness.
- **Background**: clean / matte / greenscreen → trivial matting with our MatAnyone2/BiRefNet nodes.
- **Releases**: each subject signs an ML-training + biometric-processing consent (makes the data fully owned and dual-licensable).

**Volume**: 20-50 subjects × several takes, combined with §6.17's open datasets (>1K surround scenes already), comfortably clears the single-GPU fine-tune floor (§6.13/§6.17). The own-capture portion can be as small as ~10-20 subjects if used only for domain alignment on top of the open pre-train.

## 20.3 Why this variant is a good product bet

- **2 frontal cameras > monocular** where it matters: real stereo depth and extra coverage of the face/front-of-garment (the part viewers scrutinize), while still being a trivial 2-camera shoot.
- **Back accepted as generated**: honest scope (matches the §6.13/red-team landing-page note — disclaimer that the back is AI-inferred). No physical way around it without rear cameras; the user explicitly accepts hallucinating it.
- **Strong precedent**: §8.1.1's N_ref ablation shows 1→2 reference views jumps 25.2→28.6 dB — the second frontal camera is a large, cheap quality gain.
- **Single-GPU feasible**: this is §6.13 S2 with the reference set fixed to a frontal pair — ~5-8 calendar weeks on the RTX 6000 Pro, on ungated data.
