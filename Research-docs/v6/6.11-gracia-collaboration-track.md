## Executive Summary

Gracia AI (gracia.ai, London) runs a production 4DGS volumetric-video pipeline: cloud reconstruction from multi-camera capture, a proprietary streaming-optimized format (**MINT**), and playback on Quest/Pico/WebXR/Unity/Unreal **[verified]**. The owner has an active, contract-backed relationship with them since 2025 (processing contract signed 2025-08-05) **[vault]**, and already floated the core Track B idea to CEO Georgii Vysotskii on 2026-04-13: *train our generative model on COLMAP data + ready-made 4DGS that Gracia generates* **[vault]**.

**Track B in one sentence**: Gracia turns our (and partners') capture data into highly optimized 4DGS reconstructions; we use those reconstructions as **training targets / distillation data** so our feed-forward generator (Fast Mode, Section 6.2) learns to emit similarly optimized 4DGS directly — and as an **evaluation anchor** for Quality-Mode outputs (Section 6.8). This is the standard LRM recipe (optimization-based ground truth → feed-forward student), with Gracia replacing/augmenting our planned in-house gsplat GT stage (Section 6.0 training pipeline).

**Why it's attractive**: their reconstructions are tuned for exactly the property we lack a recipe for — compact, streamable, temporally coherent splats (~1 GB/min raw → keyframe+delta streaming) **[verified]** — versus our 10-20 MB/10s target which is an extrapolation, not a result (6.0 audit note). **Why it's bounded**: Gracia is protective of format internals and raw exports **[vault]**, will likely not allow open-sourcing of anything derived from their outputs **[proposal]**, and has the in-house ML talent to build a generator themselves (CTO Andrey Volodin, ex-Prisma) **[verified]**. Hence: closed Track B branch, hard IP firewall, explicit kill-criteria, Track A continues regardless.

## 11.2 Distillation Pipeline Design (Track B technical)

The v6 training pipeline already contains a "Ground Truth 4DGS Generation (per-subject optimization with gsplat)" stage (6.0). Track B swaps in / adds Gracia reconstructions as a second, higher-grade GT source:

```
Capture (our planned 90-cam rig and/or partner stages e.g. DNE/Effigy-class)
  ├─► Track A GT: in-house gsplat per-subject optimization  (unconditional, open recipe)
  └─► Track B GT: Gracia cloud reconstruction (.mint / per-frame splats)   [closed branch]
            ↓
      Conversion: Gracia output → canonical per-frame Gaussian tensors + temporal links
            ↓
      Supervision of Fast-Mode feed-forward LRM (6.2)  +  eval anchor for Quality Mode (6.8)
```

**How the supervision actually works** [proposal, grounded in literature]:

1. **Primary signal = rendering loss, not parameter regression.** Gaussian parameterizations are many-to-one (different splat sets render identically), so the field-standard recipe is to render the GT reconstruction from dense novel views and supervise the student with image losses (L1+LPIPS+DISTS, our 6.1 stack). Precedents: LRM (arXiv 2311.04400) and GS-LRM (arXiv 2404.19702) train feed-forward reconstructors against multi-view renderings; this is exactly "distill optimization-based reconstruction into a feed-forward net."
2. **Secondary signal = parameter-space distillation** where Gracia's optimization encodes what we want to imitate: splat **count/budget**, opacity sparsity, temporal keyframe+delta structure. Direct supervision on fitted-Gaussian assets has precedent in GVGEN (arXiv 2403.12957) and GaussianCube (arXiv 2403.19655), which fit Gaussians per object and train generative models on the fitted representations; NVIDIA's Lyra (nv-tlabs/lyra — already discussed with Gracia by the owner, 2026-04) distills a generative model into 3DGS output. Requires raw (pre-quantization) splats per frame from Gracia.
3. **Compression-target distillation**: if Gracia exposes the MINT keyframe/delta layout (even as a black-box encoder + size/quality report), we can train our compression stage (6.6, MEGA+P-4DGS-inspired) to match their rate-distortion curve as a benchmark — without ever shipping their code.
4. **Eval anchoring**: Quality-Mode (6.8) outputs scored against Gracia reconstructions of the same capture (PSNR/LPIPS on held-out cameras, flicker metrics, MB/s). This alone is valuable even if no training-data deal happens.

**What we need from Gracia, in descending order of value** [proposal]:
- **(a)** Per-frame raw Gaussian parameters (position, rotation, scale, opacity, SH/color) *before* their final quantization, per clip — the distillation gold.
- **(b)** Temporal correspondences (which Gaussians persist across frames / keyframe-delta mapping) — needed to supervise our DCT/GNN motion tiers (6.3).
- **(c)** Camera calibration convention + coordinate frame docs, so conversion is lossless.
- **(d)** MINT spec **or** a sanctioned exporter ("classic splats at time t" — exactly the Irrealix request) under NDA.
- **(e)** Batch-processing access (API or managed) at a dataset-scale price, not per-client retail.
- Fallback if (a)/(b) refused: PLY-sequence export (they have done PLY deliverables before, e.g. the Blackmagic proposal listed "full .PLY sequence" [vault]) — loses temporal structure but still usable for rendering-loss supervision and eval.

**Scale needed** [proposal]: for fine-tuning/distillation on top of our own GT (not replacing it), order of 50-200 processed minutes is a meaningful pilot. At the vault-indicated €800-2500/min retail this is €40k-500k — which is why the deal must be barter/partner-priced, not retail (see 11.4).

## 11.3 IP & Firewall Structure (protecting Track A)

**Principles** [proposal — for IP counsel, budget already exists in 6.7]:

1. **Two repos, two buckets, two run namespaces.** Track B gets a separate private repo, separate storage (no shared paths with the open-source branch), separate experiment tracking. Nothing Gracia-derived — data, format docs, converter code written against their NDA'd spec, model checkpoints trained on their outputs — ever enters the Track A repo or the public ComfyUI nodes.
2. **People firewall (lightweight clean-room).** Whoever reads the MINT spec under NDA does not write Track A compression code (extends the 6.7 clean-room machinery we already run for Flex4DHuman — a new research source, same discipline). Generic knowledge ("keyframe+delta works") is unprotectable and fine; their specific layouts are not.
3. **Model-rights clause is the whole negotiation.** Default contract patterns to propose:
   - *We own the trained weights*; Gracia gets a field-of-use carve-out or rev-share on products embedding those weights (Track B products only).
   - Gracia's outputs are licensed to us as **training data** under a data-processing/data-license agreement that explicitly permits ML training and derivative-model commercialization — silence on this point is a future lawsuit.
   - Explicit statement that **no Gracia IP is embodied in the weights** (weights learn from renders/parameters, contain no Gracia code) — but contract certainty beats theory.
4. **Field-of-use split that avoids head-on conflict** [proposal]: Gracia keeps capture-to-playback services and enterprise volumetric production; we keep generative/feed-forward avatar creation and ComfyUI distribution; joint offering where they overlap.
5. **Termination hygiene.** Pre-agree: on partnership end, (i) we keep a perpetual, paid-up license to weights already trained (their preferred alternative — weight destruction — is a kill-criterion for the deal); (ii) Gracia-derived raw data is deleted/returned; (iii) eval results remain usable. Track A is untouched by construction.
6. **Open-source statement for Track A**: the open branch trains only on our own gsplat GT and openly licensed data; its release notes can truthfully say "no partner-proprietary data or formats were used." This sentence must stay true — it is the firewall's acceptance test.

## 11.4 Deal Options

What we bring (anchored in the existing relationship, all [vault] unless noted):
- **Capture data**: the planned 90-cam/4K/60fps rig (6.0) produces exactly the clean multi-view footage their pipeline monetizes; plus the owner's rig-building expertise and partner stages he brokers (DNE intro flow already exists both ways).
- **Distribution**: ComfyUI channel reaching generative-AI users Gracia does not currently touch; owner's LinkedIn reach (~15k/post engagement noted in vault) already markets 4DGS for them.
- **Deal-flow history**: 2 years of mutual intros (Dorier, Sixtrees, Plimsoll, Arcortex, Univ. Arizona, Netflix VFX contacts...) — we are an established channel partner, not a cold caller.

| Model | Shape | Pros / Cons [proposal] |
|---|---|---|
| **B1. Per-clip processing fee** | Pay partner-rate per processed minute (target ≤30-50% of the €800-2500 retail) for a defined pilot dataset | Simple, fast, clean IP. Cash cost; no Gracia upside → weakest alignment |
| **B2. Data-for-service barter** | We deliver rig capture sessions / new-client captures; they process our training corpus in exchange | Near-zero cash; deepens existing contract. Requires our rig operational — timing risk |
| **B3. Rev-share on Track B product** | Closed "optimized 4DGS generation" ComfyUI/enterprise product; Gracia gets X% of revenue + branding ("optimized to Gracia standard") | Best alignment, matches the owner's 2026-04-06 idea (e.g. optimizing Lyra-style generative output through their stack). Hardest to negotiate; needs the model-rights clause first |
| **B4. Joint enterprise offering** | Bundled pitch: their capture/processing + our generative avatars for studios (Netflix-VFX-class leads already in the pipe) | Immediate revenue story; doesn't by itself get us training data |

Recommended opening **[proposal]**: B2 + B1 hybrid for a 20-50 minute pilot (barter where possible, partner-rate cash for the gap), with B3 as the framed end-state and the model-rights clause negotiated *in the pilot agreement*, not deferred.

## 11.5 Risks & Kill-Criteria

| Risk | Assessment | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **They build the generator themselves** | Real: Volodin is a strong ML engineer (ex-Prisma) [verified]; our idea is already disclosed in outline [vault] | Move fast on the pilot; secure model-rights early; our moat = clean rig data + ComfyUI channel + feed-forward research depth, not the idea itself |
| Dependency creep | Track B conveniences leak into Track A plans | Firewall (11.3); Track A GT stage (gsplat) is never descoped |
| Raw-splat access refused | Likely first answer, given the Irrealix/4DV precedent [vault] | Fallback to PLY-sequence + rendering-loss-only distillation; if even that fails → eval-anchor-only relationship |
| GT quality unknowns | Their temporal coherence/flicker at dataset scale is a marketing claim until measured | Pilot acceptance test: 2-3 clips, held-out-camera PSNR/LPIPS + flicker metric vs our gsplat GT before any volume commitment |
| Pricing makes dataset uneconomical | Retail €800-2500/min × needed minutes is prohibitive | Barter (B2); cap pilot spend (~€15-25k [proposal]) |
| Their data terms poison our weights | NC-style or "no ML training" clauses on outputs | Non-negotiable: training rights in writing or no Track B |

**Kill-criteria (any one ends Track B; none affects Track A):**
1. No written ML-training + derivative-model commercialization rights on their outputs within the pilot contract.
2. No usable export path (raw splats or at minimum PLY sequence + calibration) within ~6 weeks of pilot start.
3. Pilot quality test: Gracia GT fails to beat our in-house gsplat GT on the agreed metrics (then they add cost, not quality).
4. Exclusivity demands that constrain Track A's open-source release or our choice of other partners.
5. Termination terms requiring destruction of already-trained weights.

**Unconditional statement**: Track A — own capture, own gsplat GT, own clean-room models, open-source-compatible release — proceeds on the 6.0/6.8 roadmap regardless of any Track B outcome. Track B can only add a higher-grade GT source and a commercial branch; it can never be on Track A's critical path.
