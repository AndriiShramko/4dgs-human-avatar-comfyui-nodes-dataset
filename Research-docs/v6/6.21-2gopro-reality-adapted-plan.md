## 21.0 Conceptual clarification (the owner's question, answered)

**At inference**: any camera view you actually provide as a *reference* is used as-is → that region is faithful (real). Any view you do NOT provide is *generated* by the model. With 2 frontal GoPros, you never provide a back view → **the back is always hallucinated**. ✅ the owner's understanding is correct.

**Three nuances**:
1. The model can only hallucinate the back *well* if it was **trained on real backs**. That training signal comes from **open surround datasets** (§6.17: PKU-DyMVHumans, CMU Panoptic, AIST++ — hundreds of subjects filmed by cameras all around), **NOT from the owner's 2 cameras**.
2. The model does not "use" a back camera automatically — only views explicitly fed as reference inputs condition the output.
3. Therefore the owner's 5 people / 2 GoPros are **not** the back-learning source. They are: (a) real product input (2 frontal clips); (b) **360° turntable scans** that give real per-subject all-around appearance for domain adaptation + validation.

## 21.2 Capture protocol (2× GoPro, no studio)

**Rigid rig** — mount both Hero 13 on ONE fixed bracket/bar → constant relative pose → **calibrate once** with a charuco/checkerboard pass (intrinsics + extrinsics); recalibrate only if the bracket is disturbed. Geometry for "2 frontal, more front detail": both at chest height (~1.4 m), **baseline ~20–40 cm**, **~2–2.5 m** from subject, slight toe-in convergence. Small baseline = less sync-sensitivity + clean front stereo, never sees the back.

**Matting (no green screen)** — our existing ComfyUI nodes (SAM2 / BiRefNet / MatAnyone2) do video matting on arbitrary/cluttered backgrounds; that is exactly their purpose. Per clip: video matte → temporally-consistent alpha (MatAnyone2) → keep subject only. Pitfalls: motion blur, fine hair, subject-colored backgrounds → mitigate with decent light and by not standing in front of skin/clothing-colored walls. Varied locations are fine because the background is discarded.

**360° turntable scan = the back-GT trick (per subject)** — subject holds a **fixed pose** and rotates slowly 360° (lazy-Susan, office chair, or just turning in place) in front of the fixed 2-GoPro rig. Across the turn both cameras observe **all azimuths incl. the back**. Recover per-frame relative poses with COLMAP or a foundation reconstructor (VGGT/MASt3R) treating it as an orbit → a real multi-view set of that subject. Caveat: **quasi-static** (rigid pose) → gives back **appearance**, not dynamics. Do **3–5 poses/outfits per subject**. Separately capture **dynamic 2-frontal performance clips** (the real product input).

**Per-subject, varied-location checklist** (×~5 people):
- Labs QR/GPS sync → clap → record.
- 3–5 **turntable scans** (different poses/outfits; aim for **varied backs**: jackets, hair up/down, logos/prints, backpack).
- 3–5 **dynamic 2-frontal clips** (10–20 s: gestures, arms, step-in-place, talking).
- Charuco board shot once per rig setup.
- Natural light OK; optionally shoot a grey-ball / phone-HDRI for relighting.
- Signed ML-training + biometric consent.

## 21.3 Adapted training plan

**Stage A — back-learning (the real generalization), open data, on RTX 6000 Pro**: fine-tune Wan-1.3B + PRoPE (§8.1.1) on **open surround datasets** (PKU-DyMVHumans / CMU Panoptic / AIST++, §6.17), with the **reference set fixed to a frontal pair whose relative pose matches the GoPro rig geometry** (train/inference alignment). This is §6.13 **S2 (~5–8 calendar weeks)** — where the back is actually learned from hundreds of real subjects.

**Stage B — domain adaptation (own data), light LoRA, days**: adapt to the GoPro look + exact rig with the 5-subject **turntable scans** (as small per-subject multi-view sets: reference = the 2 frontal-azimuth frames, target = other azimuths incl. back) + GoPro clips.

**Validation**: held-out clips of the 5 real subjects, **front and back scored separately** (front = fidelity; back = plausibility + temporal stability).

**Honest expectation**: 5 subjects **cannot** train a robust general model — generalization comes from Stage A's open-data pre-train; the 5 people are adaptation + validation only. **MVP success = faithful front + plausible, flicker-free back on the owner's 5 real subjects.**

**Sync-risk on data**: trust **turntable** scans (quasi-static, robust to sync error) as appearance GT; treat **dynamic GoPro clips** mainly as product-input demos + front-fidelity, not as precise stereo GT for fast motion.

**Firewall (§6.12)**: NC weights / open-NC data never enter commercial Track A.

## 21.8 Independent 6-agent re-audit of v2 (2026-06-16) — findings for v3

Six narrow specialists re-audited the §21.7 v2 prompt. They CONFIRMED all v2 architecture numbers and the 7 §21.6 fixes, and surfaced material NEW issues to fold into **v3**. Severity in brackets.

### Architecture (DiT/PRoPE)
- **[MED] Missing text/caption/CFG path.** §8.1.1 uses Gemini captions + CFG-dropout 0.1 + inference CFG 3.0. v2 has none → Wan text-cross-attn undefined at CFG=3.0. Add appearance-captions + CFG-dropout, OR explicit null-text regime.
- **[MED-HIGH] Stage-A must be FULL-FT, not LoRA.** Paper full-FT'd all stages; low-rank LoRA can't learn the new conditioning modality + recover repartitioned RoPE. Full-FT (or high-rank on QKV+input-proj+time-embed) for Stage-A; reserve low-rank LoRA for Stage-B only.
- **[LOW] Wording:** RoPE/PRoPE are parameter-free — say "input proj 16→36 trainable + attention QKV adaptable; PE bands active from stage 1", not "trainable PE wiring". CONFIRMED: 36-ch, RoPE 16/8/20/42/42, SE(3), flow-matching, VAE-16ch, FP8→BF16.

### Camera/sync/optics
- **[MED-HIGH] Lock exposure/WB/ISO (Protune fixed Kelvin, pinned ISO, fixed shutter).** GoPro auto-exposure/WB differ between the 2 bodies + frame-to-frame → breaks stereo matching, matting, appearance GT. Absent from v2.
- **[MED-HIGH] Disable HyperSmooth stabilization + Horizon Lock/Leveling** — they warp/crop per frame → invalidate the "calibrate once" intrinsics.
