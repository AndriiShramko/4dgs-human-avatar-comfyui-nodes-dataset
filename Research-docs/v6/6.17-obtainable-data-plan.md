## Executive Summary

1. **Access taxonomy is the whole game.** Three gate classes: **(O) open** = `wget`/script/HF-public, no human in the loop; **(C) click-through** = self-serve agree-to-terms or email-the-author-a-signed-PDF, *any* individual qualifies; **(I) institutional** = manual approval requiring an institutional email / PI. **Only class I is disqualifying.** The prior plans died on class I (DNA-Rendering, ActorsHQ, and — newly confirmed here — *all* ETH-AIT sets and RenderMe-360). Classes O and C are fully solo-obtainable.
2. **The cheapest no-gating path is real-O + synthetic, no email required:** **CMU Panoptic Studio** (open `getData.sh`, 31 HD + 480 VGA cams, NC) + **AIST++** annotations (CC BY 4.0, open) + our **CC0/Apache Blender synthetic stack** (§6.10). This trio needs zero forms and zero approvals. Add class-C sets (PKU-DyMVHumans, THuman2.1, 2K2K) by a same-day click-through/email to multiply volume.
3. **Volume is sufficient for a single-GPU fine-tune.** Comparable Wan-class fine-tunes used **0.5K–13.6K scenes** (§6.10 §10.5). The obtainable mix yields **>1K genuine multi-view scenes** before synthesis: PKU-DyMVHumans (45 scenarios × 56–60 cams), CMU Panoptic (~65+ released multi-cam sequences), HUMBI (772 subjects × 107 cams), AIST++ (1,408 seqs × 9 views), plus THuman2.1 (2,500 scans renderable to *unlimited* virtual views) and a 1–3K-scene Blender set. This clears the 500-scene floor by a wide margin even excluding any class-I data.
4. **The two best *actually-downloadable real* datasets verified**: **(1) CMU Panoptic Studio** — the only large synchronized multi-view human set with a fully open script download [A]; **(2) PKU-DyMVHumans** — 56–60-cam 1080p/4K dynamic-human video, NC, on HF (click-through) + Baidu [A/B], the closest license-clean stand-in for DNA-Rendering's role as 4DGS ground-truth.
5. **Recommended NC-MVP mix**: real-O backbone (Panoptic + AIST++) as the domain anchor → class-C densifiers (PKU-DyMVHumans for dense-view 4DGS GT; THuman2.1/2K2K scans for appearance + unlimited synthetic views) → Blender synthetic (§6.10) for perfect SE(3)/depth/mask supervision and pose coverage → owner mini-rig (§6.10 §10.3) for the sim-to-real bridge. Every shard carries a **provenance manifest** (source, license, gate-class, date).
6. **Residual risk**: no gated-data PSNR anchor (the paper's 25.44 dB used gated DNA-Rendering test set) → must re-baseline gates on a held-out Panoptic/PKU split (§6.13 red-team item already flagged this). HUMBI's formal license text is **[UNVERIFIED]**.

## 17.2 Self-Rendered Synthetic Plan (expands §6.10 §10.1)

The §6.10 CC0/Apache/MIT stack is reused verbatim and is the *only* component with **perfect, free SE(3)+depth+mask** labels and unlimited camera count — the exact supervision the multi-view DiT and 4DGS GT need.

- **Assets (all $0-license, verified §6.10):** MakeHuman exports (**CC0**), Anny native topology (**Apache 2.0**), Microsoft Rocketbox (**MIT**), Poly Haven HDRIs/environments/textures (**CC0**, AI-training explicitly welcomed), CMU mocap (free, commercial OK). Clothing: MakeHuman community CC0 garments + Blender cloth sim. **BEDLAM is recipe-only** (its SMPL-X assets/renders are MPI-NC) — replicate the *pipeline*, never touch the assets.
- **Scan-to-synthetic bridge (NC track only):** THuman2.1 (SMPL-X) and 2K2K (SMPL-X) scans, once obtained via class-C, can be **rigged and re-rendered** to unlimited virtual cameras — turning ~4,550 static scans into an arbitrary-view supervision pool with exact cameras. (Keep firewalled from commercial Track A, which uses only the CC0/Apache assets.)
- **Pipeline:** MakeHuman/Anny + scans → CMU-mocap retarget → cloth sim → Poly Haven HDRI/env → **Cycles render 10–36 virtual cams on rig-matched trajectories** → perfect SE(3)/depth/mask/normal. Our 72 preprocessing nodes composite synthetic foregrounds over real backgrounds for domain randomization.
- **Render time on the 96 GB card [estimate]:** 1–3K scenes × 16 cams × 81 frames @512² ≈ **2.6–13 GPU-days** of Cycles on the RTX 6000 Pro (extrapolating §6.10's "$1–3K spot-cloud" to a single local Blackwell; Cycles is throughput-bound, the 96 GB removes any scene-size limit). VAE-encode of the renders adds the §6.13 1–3 day one-off. Runs in parallel with class-C download/extraction.

## 17.4 Recommended NC-MVP Data Mix + Provenance Manifest

| Tier | Source | Gate | Role | Share [estimate] |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Real anchor (no-form) | CMU Panoptic + AIST++(+DanceDB) | O / O+C | Domain realism, view-pairs, sparse-view cond. | 35% |
| Dense 4DGS GT | PKU-DyMVHumans (HUMBI as backup) | C / O | 56–60-cam dense supervision = DNA-Rendering's role | 20% |
| Scan→synthetic | THuman2.1 + 2K2K re-rendered | C | Unlimited virtual views + SMPL-X GT | 15% |
| Pure synthetic | CC0/Apache Blender stack (§6.10) | O | Perfect SE(3)/depth/mask, pose coverage | 20% |
| Own captures | 8–12 cam mini-rig | owned | Sim-to-real bridge + real test set | 10% |

- **Generative share ≤20–30%** of any mix, real anchor always present (model-collapse policy, §6.10 §10.2).
- **Provenance manifest (one row per training shard):** `{source, version, gate_class, license, license_url, obtained_date, redistributable: bool, commercial_ok: false}`. CI rejects any shard lacking a manifest row or tagged gate-class **I**. This is the §6.10 asset-allowlist made enforceable and the §6.12 Track-A firewall's ingestion guard.
