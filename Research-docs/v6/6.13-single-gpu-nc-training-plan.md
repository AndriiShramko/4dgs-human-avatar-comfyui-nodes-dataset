## 13.2 Replication Cost Scaling (show-your-math)

**Token counts** (Wan VAE /8 spatial, /4 temporal; patchify 2×2): 256² → 32² latent → 16² = **256 tokens/latent-frame/view**; 512² → **1,024 tokens**. So: stage 1 (2 views @256², T=1) **L=512**; stage 2.1 (V=16 @256²) **L=4,096**; stage 2.2 (V=16 @512²) **L=16,384**; stage 3 (512², layouts {32×1…2×32}, T≤32→≤9 latent frames) **L≈16k–33k, avg ~24k**.

**Per-sample training FLOPs** for Wan-1.3B (30 blocks, d=1536 [B]): F(L) ≈ 6·P·L (linear) + 12·L²·d·30 (attention, fwd 4L²d/layer ×3 for bwd) = 7.8e9·L + 5.53e5·L²:
- L=512 → 4.1e12 · L=2,048 → 1.8e13 · L=4,096 → 4.1e13 · L=8,192 → 1.0e14 · L=16,384 → 2.8e14 · L=24,576 → 5.3e14

**Their total** [estimate — assumes effective batch 32 = 1/GPU × 32, ReCamMaster precedent; batch unpublished]:
3.9e18 (S1: 30k×32×4.1e12) + 3.9e19 (S2.1) + 2.7e20 (S2.2) + 2.2e20 (S3: 15k×32×4.5e14) ≈ **5.3e20 FLOPs**.
At H100 BF16 × 25% MFU (2.5e14 FLOP/s): floor ≈ **590 H100-hours** (≈18 h wall on 32×H100). Real-world (mixed layouts, comms, 10–15% MFU): **~1,200–2,500 H100-h**; if their batch was 64–128: **up to ~10,000 H100-h** (~2 weeks wall — plausible for a lab run). Working range: **1,000–10,000 H100-hours**.

**Card sustained throughput** [estimate]: FP8 1.0 PF dense × 15–25% effective MFU = **1.5–2.5e14 FLOP/s** (central 1.5e14). Sanity anchor: community Wan-1.3B LoRA on RTX 3090 = 2.57 s/iter measured (§9.1 ref 1) vs 0.57 s ideal → ~14% end-to-end MFU; we assume the same class of overhead.

**(a) Naive full replication**: 5.3e20 / 1.5e14 = 3.5e6 s = **41 days pure GPU at the floor**; with realistic overhead ×2–3 and batch uncertainty ×1–4 → **2–6 months, worst case ~12**. **Rejected.**

**(b) Reduced replication (full FT)** — justified cuts for our narrower goal (humans-only, 1–8 ref views, V=8 output suffices to seed 4DGS fitting; dense coverage recovered by a second conditioned pass at inference): V=8 above stage 1 (halves L → ¼ attention, ½ linear), iters 105k→65k (15k+20k+20k+10k), T≤16, batch 16:
9.8e17 + 5.9e18 + 3.2e19 + 2.9e19 ≈ **6.8e19 FLOPs** → 5.3 days floor, ×2–3 overhead → **~11–16 days pure GPU** (range 8–25).

**(c) LoRA/PEFT variant**: rank 64–128 on q/k/v/o + FFN of all 30 blocks (~80–150M trainable) **plus dense training of the expanded 16→36ch input projection** (new zero-init channels must learn; tiny). The RoPE→(time, view, SE(3), h, w) repartition and PRoPE are **parameter-free** — nothing to train there, which is what makes LoRA plausible for this architecture at all. Per-iter compute ≈ full FT (full backprop through the frozen base); savings are optimizer memory (§13.3) and tolerance of smaller effective batch (8). Quality evidence: ReCamMaster chose full FT at this scale; community Wan-1.3B LoRAs reliably learn camera/style control but with softer pose-following precision — expect **an additional −1 to −2 dB vs full FT at equal iters** [estimate, weak evidence base]. Hence the S2 hybrid: LoRA for the cheap early curriculum, merge, full-FT only the expensive final 10–15k iters.

## 13.3 VRAM Feasibility on 96 GB

Full FT, 1.3B params [estimate, standard accounting]:
- Statics, BF16+AdamW: weights 2.6 + grads 2.6 + FP32 m/v 10.4 + FP32 master 5.2 ≈ **21 GB** (8-bit Adam → ~13 GB; LoRA → ~4–6 GB).
- Activations with full gradient checkpointing (store 30 block inputs, FlashAttention/SDPA = no L² memory): 30·L·1536·2 B ≈ **1.5 GB/sample at L=16,384** + ~1–2 GB recompute workspace.
- VAE and text encoders: **zero at train time** (precomputed, §13.4).

| Config | Statics | Per-sample acts | Max micro-batch [estimate] |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full FT, V=16 @256² (L=4k) | 21 GB | ~0.5 GB | **32+** |
| Full FT, V=16 @512² (L=16k) | 21 GB | ~3–4 GB | **4–8** ⚠️ |
| Full FT, stage-3 layouts (L≈33k) | 21 GB | ~6–8 GB | **4–6** |
| LoRA, V=8 @512² (L=8k) | ~6 GB | ~2 GB | **24+** |

> ⚠️ [red-team 2026-06-16: micro-batch "8–12" at L=16k is optimistic. The acts estimate (~3–4 GB/sample) counts only stored block inputs + a "1–2 GB recompute workspace", but at micro-batch 8 the *recompute* peak (one full block's attention + FFN intermediates re-materialized during backward, even with FlashAttention) plus FP8 scale/amax buffers, cuDNN workspaces, and fragmentation routinely consume 15–25 GB beyond the linear acts model. Realistic safe micro-batch at L=16k full-FT on 96 GB is **4–8**, with 8 only after an empirical binary search (which §6.14's queue rightly does). Use 4–8 in planning; the calendar already assumes more accumulation steps. Table value corrected to 4–8.]

Conclusion: 96 GB allows **full FT at the paper's largest token counts with micro-batch 4–8**; gradient accumulation ×2–8 reproduces effective batch 16–32. A 24 GB card cannot even hold the 21 GB statics (hence musubi block-swap tricks); 48 GB gives micro-batch 1–2 at L=16k — viable but 4–6× more accumulation steps of overhead. **This card's VRAM, not its FLOPs, is its real edge.**

**256 GB RAM**: the entire training set as latents fits in memory — 1,038 seqs × 48 cams × ~150 frames at 512²: 16×64×64×2 B = 131 KB/latent-frame, ÷4 temporal → ~250 GB BF16, **~125 GB in FP8 storage** → full hot in-RAM dataset, zero dataloader bottleneck (critical on a single card where any stall is unhidden). Also hosts CPU-side EMA weights and optimizer offload if we ever need micro-batch headroom.

BUT: cross-view consistency of few-step *multi-view* students is unpublished (§9.8 Q1). **MVP decision: skip distillation; ship 40 steps + CFG 3.0 offline with TeaCache (~2×, training-free).** Revisit as a post-MVP 1-week run; consistency/CFG-distill (halve steps, drop CFG pass) is the cheaper intermediate (~2–3 machine-days [estimate]).

## 13.7 Practical Single-Box Recipe

1. **Stack**: PyTorch ≥2.7 / CUDA 12.8 (sm_120), FlashAttention via SDPA-cuDNN backend; TransformerEngine FP8 (E4M3 fwd / E5M2 bwd) — verified supported on Blackwell workstation class [B]; fallback torchao float8 or BF16 + `fp8_base` weights.
2. **Precompute everything** (DiffSynth/musubi pattern, §9.1): VAE latents + text embeddings to disk, FP8-packed hot set in the 256 GB RAM; no VAE/T5 in the train loop.
3. **torch.compile** with static shapes (~1.15–1.3× [estimate]) — enabled by **sequence packing**: pack mixed V×T stage-3 layouts to a fixed ~16k-token budget per micro-batch so shapes never change.
4. **Memory-ordered curriculum**: V=16 only at 256² (cheap tokens); at 512² cap V=8 and recover dense coverage at inference via a second conditioned pass / rollout — aligned with the 1–8 input-view product goal.
5. Gradient checkpointing always on; grad accumulation to effective batch 16–32; 8-bit Adam if pushing micro-batch; EMA on CPU.
6. Checkpoint every 500 iters + auto-resume (single box = no fault tolerance from redundancy); fixed eval pack (4 held-out DNA seqs, paper's fg-PSNR protocol) every 2k iters.
7. Inference: 40 steps CFG 3.0 + TeaCache; T=4 chunks O=1 rollout (paper shows T=4 ≈ T=16 quality, §8.1.1 — the memory-friendly point).
