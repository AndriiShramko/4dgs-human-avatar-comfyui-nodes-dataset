> **Added**: 2026-06-12
> **Prepared by**: Shramko Research Team (deep-research agent)
> **Scope**: Closes the critical-path gap identified in §8.4 ("Data strategy (critical path)") — the 90-camera rig is not yet built; we need clean data **now** for the Wan 2.1 1.3B multi-view fine-tune (Quality Mode) and 4DGS fitting/supervision.

## Executive Summary

1. **A 100% commercially clean, $0-license synthetic stack exists**: MakeHuman-exported bodies (**CC0**) / Anny (**Apache 2.0**) + CMU mocap (**commercial use permitted**) + Poly Haven HDRIs/environments (**CC0, AI training explicitly welcomed**) + Microsoft Rocketbox (**MIT**) rendered in **Blender** (render outputs unencumbered). This covers curriculum **stage 1 (pose following)** with unlimited cameras and perfect SE(3) labels. Marginal cost ≈ render compute only.
2. **Most commercial 3D-people vendors prohibit ML training by default** (RenderPeople, Reallusion standard, MetaHuman/Fab, Mixamo). RenderPeople and Reallusion both sell explicit ML-training permission via separate/enterprise licenses — quotes required. AXYZ terms could not be retrieved — written confirmation required before any purchase.
3. **Academic synthetic datasets are NOT a shortcut**: BEDLAM, AGORA, SynBody, AMASS are all non-commercial (MPI/CC BY-NC). Do not touch for production weights.
4. **Real multi-view data for stages 2-3** is cheapest via a **self-built 8-12 camera consumer rig** (~$4-8K hardware *(estimate)*): iPhones + Tentacle Sync timecode via the Blackmagic Camera app (frame-accurate, verified) — directly matching the owner's existing 40-80-iPhone + Blackmagic genlock software experience. Subjects sign our own ML-training + biometric releases → data is fully owned, dual-licensable (free CC BY-NC release + enterprise tier), exactly the asset class §8.2 says wins.
5. **No off-the-shelf vendor of multi-view human video with ML-training rights was found.** Closest: DataSet Shop/vAIsual (studio images/video of released identities, commercial ML rights, extended license for generative training) — useful as appearance-diversity supplement, not as multi-view supervision.
6. **Data volumes are small**: comparable multi-view video-diffusion fine-tunes used 0.5K-13.6K scenes (SynCamMaster: 500 UE scenes × 36 cams; ReCamMaster: 13.6K scenes × 10 cams; Diffuman4D: one 60-camera dataset, ~1500 subjects). A synthetic stage-1 set of ~1-3K scenes × 10-36 virtual cameras is within a single workstation/cheap-cloud render budget.

**Cheapest viable clean path: ~$8-15K total *(estimate)* — $1-3K synthetic rendering + $4-8K consumer rig + $2-4K capture sessions/releases — versus waiting for the 90-camera rig.**

**Key insight**: BEDLAM's *pipeline* (procedural bodies + mocap + clothing sim + HDRI lighting + path-traced rendering) is exactly reproducible with the CC0/Apache stack above. The license poison is in MPI's *assets* (SMPL-X bodies, their renders), not in the recipe.

### Recommended synthetic pipeline (Blender, $0 licenses)

MakeHuman/Anny bodies (randomized age/weight/height) → CMU mocap retarget → cloth via Blender sim or CC0 garments → Poly Haven HDRI + environment → Cycles render from 10-36 virtual cameras on rig-matched trajectories → **perfect SE(3), depth, masks for free**. Blender is GPL but its rendered outputs are the user's — no license propagation. Render cost for ~2K scenes × 16 cams × 81 frames at 480p-720p: roughly $1-3K on spot GPU cloud or weeks on local RTX hardware *(estimate)*. Our 72 preprocessing nodes then composite synthetic foregrounds over real backgrounds (matting in reverse) for domain randomization.

## 10.2 Generative Synthetic Data (model-made humans)

- **Wan 2.1 (all sizes)**: Apache 2.0 (verified §8.2) — no output-use restrictions; outputs usable as training data for our fine-tune. Self-distillation from the *same* backbone is the degenerate case — see collapse risk below.
- **FLUX.1-schnell**: Apache 2.0 (HF model card black-forest-labs/FLUX.1-schnell) — commercially clean image generator for appearance diversity (texture/identity variation for synthetic characters via img2img/reference). Note: FLUX.1-dev is non-commercial — use schnell only.
- **Legal caveat (unchanged from §8.2)**: Apache 2.0 covers the *model*; the upstream training-data provenance of Wan/FLUX is undisclosed. Already routed to the IP-attorney review budgeted in §6.7. US Copyright Office position: pure AI outputs are not copyrightable — fine for us (we need usability, not ownership of these intermediates).
- **Model collapse is real and documented**: Shumailov et al., *Nature* 631 (2024), "AI models collapse when trained on recursively generated data" — indiscriminate training on model-generated content causes irreversible loss of distribution tails. Mitigation per the paper and follow-ups: keep a real-data anchor in every mix, filter aggressively, never train generation-on-generation for multiple rounds.
- **Policy**: generative data ≤20-30% of any training mix *(our heuristic, not a published number)*; primarily for appearance/identity diversity, never as the sole source of multi-view geometric supervision (generators do not provide true SE(3)-consistent views — that is precisely the capability we are trying to train).

## 10.3 Low-Cost Real Multi-View Capture (pre-rig)

**How many cameras are enough?** Diffuman4D (ICCV 2025) takes **4-8 input views** and reports quality "comparable to dense reconstruction from 48 views"; its training supervision came from a **60-camera** rig (DNA-Rendering: 60 synced cams, 4096×3000, 15 fps, 1500+ subjects). SynCamMaster trained with **36 virtual cameras**; ReCamMaster with **10 per scene**. → For curriculum **stage 1** (pose-following, reference→target view pairs), 8-12 real cameras already yield 56-132 ordered view-pairs per take *(combinatorics, our calculation)*; dense >40-view coverage matters mainly for final 4DGS ground-truth fitting — that is what the 90-camera rig is for, later.

| Option | Sync method | Accuracy | Hardware cost *(estimates)* |
|---|---|---|---|
| **8-12× iPhone + Tentacle Sync E + Blackmagic Camera app** | Bluetooth timecode into file metadata; auto-multicam in Resolve | **Frame-accurate** (vendor-documented; verified via Tentacle/Newsshooter docs) | Used iPhones ~$250-450 ea + 1-2 Tentacle units ~$250-330 ea → **$3-6K** |
| 8× GoPro (Labs firmware) | QR precision-time / GPS sync, TCAL calibration | ~1-2 frames typical; cross-model offsets up to ~6 frames reported (gopro/labs discussions #830) | ~$250-350 ea → **$2-3K**; add SyncBac-class hardware TC if frame-exactness required |
| Sub-frame alignment in software | Audio clap/strobe + our stabilization nodes; optical flow refinement | Sub-frame achievable for 15-30 fps motion *(standard practice; our pipeline)* | $0 |

The owner has already shipped multi-camera rig software for a **40-80 iPhone + Blackmagic genlock** project — the iPhone option reuses proven competence. Calibration: COLMAP/VGGT on a static board pass; per-take audio-clap verification. **Every subject signs our release granting ML-training + biometric processing rights (GDPR Art. 9 explicit consent)** — this is what makes the data dual-licensable (planned free CC BY-NC release + enterprise commercial tier) and is the cheapest "clean data" of all: consent costs a signature.

## 10.4 Commercially Licensable Volumetric / 4D Data

Findings (2026-06-12): **no vendor found selling synchronized multi-view human video with explicit ML-training rights off the shelf.**

- **DataSet Shop (vAIsual)** — verified real offering: "legally clean" biometric datasets (500K+ studio images, 1500+ released identities; video sets exist); commercial ML rights included, **generative-model training requires an extended per-image license** (datasetshop.com/pricing, /license). Useful as appearance/identity diversity supplement. Pricing is per-image/configurator — obtain quote.
- **Volograms** — research datasets are **non-commercial only** (volograms.com/research-datasets). ❌
- **4DViews** — sells HOLOSYS capture *systems* and free sample clips; no ML-rights data store found. A bespoke "capture day + full IP transfer" contract with a HOLOSYS-equipped studio is plausible at **$5-20K/day *(estimate, no quote)*** — fallback if our own rig slips.
- **Twindom** — has supplied scan data to academic ML projects (LifeScans); commercial ML licensing only via direct contact; no public terms. ❓
- **RenderPeople / AXYZ 4D scanned people** — gated behind the ML licenses discussed in §10.1.

**Conclusion**: category is a negotiation, not a shop. **Per owner decision 2026-06-12 this entire category is rejected** — our own consented captures dominate on cost and rights, and the rig is the plan.

## 10.5 Data Efficiency — how little is enough (verified comparables)

| Project | Backbone / method | Training data for multi-view capability |
|---|---|---|
| SynCamMaster (ICLR'25) | Video DiT + view-sync modules | **500 UE scenes × 36 cams** (+1000-scene SynCamVideo, + monocular/multi-cam image supplements) |
| ReCamMaster (ICCV'25) | Wan 2.1 **1.3B** (same backbone as ours), full conditioning fine-tune | **13.6K UE5 scenes × 10 cams = 136K videos**; published training recipe: batch 1/GPU, 8 GPUs, lr 1e-4 |
| Diffuman4D (ICCV'25) | Spatio-temporal diffusion, 4-8 input views | One dataset: DNA-Rendering (60 cams, ~1500 subjects, 5000 seqs), heavily re-processed |
| Flex4DHuman (§8) | Wan 2.1 1.3B | DNA-Rendering + ActorsHQ (~160 + ~1500 subjects order of magnitude) **[B]** |

Implication: our stage-1 target of **~1-3K synthetic scenes + tens of real consented subjects** sits inside the demonstrated range for this exact backbone class; ReCamMaster proves a Wan-1.3B camera-conditioned fine-tune converges on purely synthetic UE-rendered data (we replicate with a license-clean Blender equivalent). **License warning**: ReCamMaster's MultiCamVideo dataset itself was rendered with UE marketplace assets and its dataset license could not be verified from this environment **[UNVERIFIED]** — given Fab's no-AI-training stance (§10.1), treat it as research reference only; do not train production weights on it.

**Augmentation multipliers using owned nodes** (each labeled *estimate*): HDRI relighting at render time (synthetic, ~free), matting-based background swaps (SeC + MatAnyone2, BiRefNet) ×5-20 effective backgrounds per take, horizontal flips + camera-subset resampling ×2-10 view-pair combinations. Net: 20-50 real subjects can behave like several hundred for stage-1 conditioning *(estimate; validate on held-out subjects)*.

## 10.7 Risk Table

| Risk | Prob. | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Synthetic-to-real domain gap stalls stage 1 | Medium | High | BEDLAM-style realism recipe (HDRI light, cloth sim, motion blur); mix 0-B real data from week 3; early eval on held-out real subjects |
| Wan/FLUX upstream data provenance challenged | Low-Med | Medium | Already in §6.7 attorney review; our *fine-tuning data* is clean and documented — provenance ledger per asset |
| Model collapse from generative data | Low (if policy held) | Medium | ≤20-30% generative share, real anchor always present (Shumailov 2024) |
| Vendor license ambiguity (AXYZ, Twindom) | High | Low | Spend nothing until written ML-training terms; default to CC0/Apache stack |
| Consumer-rig sync/calibration too loose for 4DGS GT | Medium | Medium | Use rig only for stage-1 view-pair supervision, not dense GT; Tentacle timecode + software sub-frame refinement; GT 4DGS waits for the 90-cam rig |
| GDPR/personality-rights defect in own captures | Low | High | Explicit-consent biometric release (one-time template by counsel, ~$1-2K *(estimate)*); no minors; revocation process documented |
| Accidental NC-asset contamination (BEDLAM/AMASS/Mixamo sneaking in via contractors) | Medium | High | Asset allowlist + provenance manifest per training shard; CI check on dataset ingestion |
